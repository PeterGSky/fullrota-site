What it does

Four jobs it takes off your consultants

Shift broadcast

Pick the booking. FullRota messages only the candidates who are available, qualified and cleared for that client. The first yes gets it, and everyone else is told it has gone.

Availability check

Every Sunday evening candidates are asked which days they can work. They tap an answer and the board fills itself in. Anyone who stays quiet gets one nudge on Monday morning.

Compliance chasing

Expiry dates sit on every record. Candidates are reminded ahead of time, with a link to upload the new document from their phone.

Shared inbox

One agency number for every consultant. Each message is filed against the candidate, so anyone on the desk can pick up the conversation.

Compliance

Nobody goes out on an expired check

Every safer recruitment check has a date and a status on the candidate's record. When one runs out, the teacher drops out of broadcasts and gets a message asking for the new document.

Every pre-employment check has a date and a status on the candidate's record. When one runs out, the nurse or carer drops out of broadcasts and gets a message asking for the new document.

FullRota tracks documents and chases candidates. The decision to clear someone for work stays with your compliance team.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask

How many WhatsApp numbers can I have?

Most agencies connect one main number for the whole business, so candidates always know who is messaging them.

You can connect more when it suits you, such as a number for a branch or brand, or your consultants' own WhatsApp Business numbers, one at a time. Meta allows 2 numbers until your business is verified with Meta, then 20. Each number shows its own name and logo in WhatsApp, and your team sees every conversation in one shared inbox.

Does FullRota work for our sector?

Yes. The examples on this page are education and healthcare, but FullRota is set up for each agency's specialism, whether that's nursing, teaching, social care, driving, hospitality, security or something else. We configure your roles, shift or booking patterns, compliance checks and rates, usually within a week.

Your team, candidates and clients see your sector's own words throughout, in the app and in WhatsApp messages. If you'd rather say "carer", "home" or "session", your admin can change the wording at any time, and agencies covering several sectors can use different wording for each desk.

Can we keep the WhatsApp number we already use?

Yes. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, you connect it to FullRota and keep using the app on your phone as normal. Both stay in sync, and your contacts and up to 6 months of one-to-one chats copy across once.

Personal WhatsApp numbers (the ordinary green app) need to switch to the free WhatsApp Business app first.

Can we disconnect a number later?

Yes. Click Disconnect next to the number in FullRota and it stops sending and receiving straight away. To fully unlink it from Meta, also go to Settings, then Account, then Business Platform, then Disconnect Account in the WhatsApp Business app. The app on the phone carries on working either way.

Do candidates need to download anything?

No. They use the WhatsApp they already have. Forms open inside the chat, and there is nothing to install or log in to.

Do candidates have to agree to receive messages?

Yes. Each candidate opts in before you message them, and the date is saved on their record. They can stop messages at any time.

Can other agencies on FullRota see my candidates?

No. Every agency has its own separate space. Your candidates, messages and documents are only visible to your team and to the clients you invite.

Can our schools and care homes log in?

Yes, if you want them to. Clients can see people who have worked with them before, check who is free and send an invite, which goes out on WhatsApp from your number. You are told about every invite.

Who owns our data?

You do. FullRota handles it on your behalf under a data processing agreement, and you can export or delete your data whenever you like.

What happens if we go over our message allowance?

Nothing stops working. Extra messages cost 5p each on Starter and 4p each on Growth, and you can see your usage at any time. Replies to candidates who message you first don't count towards your allowance.

Is there a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month. If you pay for a year up front, you get two months free.
