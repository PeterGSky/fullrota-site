Privacy policy

How we use personal information

This policy explains what personal information Kinetic Flow Limited collects through the FullRota website and from our customers, why we use it, and the rights you have.

Last updated 17 October 2026

Who we are

FullRota is provided by Kinetic Flow Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 17526373. Our registered office is 3 Gainsborough Court, Skipton, England, BD23 1QG. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller, registration number ZC277693.

For anything about this policy or your personal information, email hello@fullrota.com.

If an agency contacts you through FullRota

Staffing agencies use FullRota to manage their workers, clients and WhatsApp messages. When an agency does this, the agency decides how your information is used. It is the controller of that information, and we act as its processor, handling it only on the agency's instructions and under a written data processing agreement.

The agency's own privacy notice explains how it uses your information. To ask about it, or to use your rights, contact the agency directly. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to the agency.

What we collect and why

The rest of this policy covers information we decide how to use ourselves, as the controller.

When you visit this website

Our hosting provider's servers keep standard technical records of each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. We use these to run the website and keep it secure. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website.

When you contact us or book a demo

We use your name, email address, phone number, organisation and anything you tell us to reply, arrange and hold a demo, and follow up about FullRota. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and telling interested organisations about our service.

When your agency signs up

We use the names, job titles, work email addresses and phone numbers of the people who sign up and use FullRota for their agency, along with sign-in and security records. We use this to set up and provide the service, give support, keep accounts secure, and handle billing. Our lawful basis is performing our contract with your agency, and our legitimate interest in running and securing our service. We also keep records we are required to keep by law, such as for tax.

Updates and news

We may email people at agencies we work with, or who have asked about FullRota, with news about the service. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email or by emailing us. We send these only as the law allows.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use tracking technologies.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. Google's privacy policy explains how it handles this.

The question-and-answer assistant on our home page runs entirely in your browser. What you type into it is not sent to us or stored.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with organisations that help us run FullRota, and only as much as they need:

  • Hosting: DigitalOcean, which hosts this website and the FullRota service.
  • Email: the provider of our business email.
  • WhatsApp: Meta, which carries WhatsApp messages for agencies that use the FullRota WhatsApp features.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts, as described above.
  • Professional advisers such as our accountants and lawyers, where needed.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others. We do not sell personal information.

International transfers

Some of these providers are based in, or use services in, countries outside the UK, such as the United States. Where personal information leaves the UK, we make sure it is protected by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Website server records: a short period set by our hosting provider, then deleted.
  • Enquiries and demo requests: up to two years after we were last in touch, unless your organisation becomes a customer.
  • Customer account and billing records: for as long as your agency uses FullRota, then six years after the agreement ends, for legal and tax purposes.

Keeping it secure

We use encryption in transit, access controls and activity logging to protect personal information, and we limit access to people who need it to do their job.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it;
  • object to us using your information on the basis of legitimate interests, including for direct marketing;
  • ask for information you gave us to be passed to another organisation, where this applies.

To use any of these rights, email hello@fullrota.com. We will reply within one month. There is usually no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our services or the law change, and show the date of the latest version at the top of this page.
