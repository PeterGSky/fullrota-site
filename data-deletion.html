Deleting your data

How to ask us to delete personal information held in FullRota, including WhatsApp messages.

If an agency contacted you through FullRota

Staffing agencies use FullRota to manage their workers, clients and WhatsApp conversations. The agency decides what is kept about you, so the quickest way to have your details and messages deleted is to ask the agency directly.

You can also email us at hello@fullrota.com. Tell us the agency's name and the phone number you use on WhatsApp. We will pass your request to the agency and help it delete your information from FullRota.

Information we hold ourselves

To delete information we hold as Kinetic Flow Limited, for example if you contacted us or booked a demo, email hello@fullrota.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your name and the email address or phone number you used with us.

What happens next

We will confirm we have received your request, may ask you to confirm who you are, and will reply within one month. Once deleted, your information is removed from FullRota and from our backups as they expire. We may keep a small amount where the law requires it, and we will tell you if so.

If you connected a WhatsApp Business account to FullRota, disconnecting it stops any new messages reaching FullRota. Ask us if you also want past messages deleted.
